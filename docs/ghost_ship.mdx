# The Spectral Ghost Galleon

On stormy nights and during the Full Moon, an eerie green fog rolls over the open ocean as the legendary Spectral Galleon anchors offshore.

---

## 🌙 Natural Arrival Event
* **Spawn Conditions**: Occurs on **Full Moon nights** ($Day \% 8 == 0$) or during midnight thunderstorms with a 40% natural arrival chance.
* **Mooring Distance**: The ship automatically scans for deep open water **72 to 105 blocks offshore**.
* **Automatic Sunrise Despawn**: Protected throughout the nocturnal hours; automatically dematerializes into an emerald mist vortex at the first light of dawn.

---

## 🚢 Galleon Architecture
* **4-Tier Deep Keel**: Submerged 4 blocks below sea level ($y = 59..63$) with Dark Prismarine spine and Mangrove ribs.
* **Boarding Ladders**: Flush-mounted boarding ladders on Port and Starboard sides with boarding bulwark gaps.
* **Soul Illumination**: 10+ Soul Lanterns, Yardarm chains, Crows Nest, and a Bowsprit Soul Campfire.

---

## ☠️ Captain Blacktide (Boss - 125 HP)
* **Abilities**:
  * **Phantom Phase Dash**: Teleports behind opponents with a shockwave dealing 7.0 damage.
  * **Cursed Wail**: Releases a sonic wave inflicting Slowness and Darkness.
  * **Minion Summon**: Calls forth **Spectral Corsairs** to swarm the main deck.
* **Loot & Rewards**:
  * Unlocks the **Spectral Vault** chest in the Captain's Cabin.
  * Drops **Bottle of Ectoplasm** and **Cursed Pirate Doubloons**.

---

## 🕯️ Ethereal Relics
* **⚔️ Spectral Cutlass**: Right-click to trigger a **Phantom Phase Dash** dealing 8.5 soul damage.
* **🕯️ Cursed Ghost Lantern**: `3x Ectoplasm, 1x Soul Lantern, 1x Prismarine Shard`. Grants passive underwater Night Vision + Water Breathing; right-click activates a 28-block **Spectral Sonar Radar**.
