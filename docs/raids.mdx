# Pirate Sieges & Coastal Defense

Hostile pirate armadas patrol the surrounding waters. Defend your island outpost against aggressive assault raids!

---

## 🔔 The Lookout Bell
* **Recipe**: `4x Iron Ingots, 2x Oak Planks, 1x Gold Ingot`
* Ringing the bell warns of incoming pirate assault fleets and highlights approaching skiffs on the horizon.

---

## 💣 Coastal Broadside Cannons
* **Recipe**: `5x Iron Ingots, 1x Dispenser, 2x Wood Logs, 1x Flint & Steel`
* **Operation**:
  1. Load forged **Cannonballs** (`4x Iron Nuggets, 1x Gunpowder` yields 4x cannonballs) into the cannon.
  2. Right-click with Flint & Steel or trigger with Redstone to fire heavy artillery projectiles that deal massive explosive and concussive kinetic damage to incoming ships.

---

## ⚔️ Pirate Weapons
* **Pirate Cutlass**: `2x Iron Ingots, 1x Gold Nugget, 1x Stick`. Deals fast sweeping damage with a reduced recovery cooldown.
* **Molotov Cocktail**: `1x Glass Bottle, 1x Gunpowder, 1x Paper`. Throwable incendiary weapon that creates an expanding barrier of lingering fire.
