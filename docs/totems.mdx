# Ancient Tiki Totems & The Smuggler

Harness the elemental forces of ancient island gods and trade with oceanic wanderers.

---

## 🗿 Ancient Tiki Totems
* **Recipe**: `8x Stripped Oak Logs, 1x Ocean Pearl`
* **Carving**: Hold any **Cutlass** in your main hand and right-click the placed totem to cycle and carve sacred faces:
  * ☀️ **Sol (Sun Face)**: Grants Island Strength II & Mining Haste I to nearby players.
  * 🌊 **Moana (Ocean Face)**: Grants Reef Conduit Power, Water Breathing, and Dolphin's Grace.
  * ⚡ **Taranis (Storm Face)**: Calls down lightning strikes upon hostile pirate raiders who enter your perimeter.

---

## 🛶 The Wandering Smuggler
* An eccentric merchant who periodically drifts ashore on a supply raft.
* Trades rare **Cursed Pirate Doubloons** for exotic spices, diving blueprints, and rare ocean relics.
