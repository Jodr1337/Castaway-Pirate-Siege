# Castaway: Pirate Siege

**Castaway: Pirate Siege** transforms solitary island survival into a dynamic, content-packed nautical adventure for **Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric)**.

---

## Welcome, Castaway

Stranded on an isolated tropical island amidst endless dark waters, you must master the ocean to survive:
* **Salvage Drifting Flotsam**: Reel in drifting wooden cargo crates using the **Flotsam Hook**.
* **Sift Sand & Gravel**: Unearth iron nuggets, flint, clay, and rare **Ocean Pearls** with the **Sieve Basket**.
* **Defend Your Shoreline**: Construct iron **Coastal Cannons**, forge **Cannonballs**, and ring the **Lookout Bell** to repel pirate assault fleets.
* **Explore the Ghost Ship**: Brave the full moon fog to board the 21-block **Spectral Ghost Galleon**, slay **Captain Blacktide**, and claim ethereal relics.
* **Tame Island Companions**: Befriend shoulder-riding **Capuchin Monkeys** and race speedy **Beach Crabs**.
* **Carve Tiki Totems**: Channel ancient island guardian spirits for powerful area buffs.
