# Island Ecology, Traps & Crab Racing

Tropical islands are thriving with native wildlife, ocean resources, and unique activities.

---

## 🦞 Lobster Pot Traps
* **Recipe**: `4x Sticks, 4x Oak Planks, 1x String`
* **Placement**: Must be placed completely submerged in ocean water.
* **Catches**:
  * Lobsters
  * Azure, Gold, and Red Island Crabs
  * Pearl Oysters (Yields Ocean Pearls)

---

## 🦀 Beach Crab Racing
* Wild crabs scuttle along the shoreline.
* Right-click with an empty hand to pick up a crab into your inventory.
* Place crabs inside an arena track and feed them **Kelp** or **Gold Nuggets** to trigger speed sprint races with friends!

---

## 🐒 Capuchin Deckhand Monkeys
* **Taming**: Feed wild monkeys **Cracked Coconuts**.
* **Behaviors**:
  * Right-click to toggle **Sitting** / **Following**.
  * Sneak + Right-click to **Shoulder-Mount** your monkey companion!

---

## 🍲 Island Seafood Cuisine
* **Spiced Crab Boil**: `1x Cooked Crab, 1x Bowl, 1x Kelp, 1x Brown Mushroom`. Restores 8 hunger and grants Resistance I.
* **Butter Lobster Tail**: `1x Cooked Lobster, 1x Milk Bucket, 1x Honey Bottle`. Restores 10 hunger and grants Dolphin's Grace + Haste II.
