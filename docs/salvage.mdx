# Ocean Salvage & Sifting

Survival on an isolated island requires resourcefulness. Ocean currents constantly deliver flotsam and cargo from shattered vessels.

---

## 📦 Drifting Flotsam Crates
* Floating wooden barrels and supply crates drift toward the island's shores periodically.
* Loot includes food rations, seeds, rope, timber, metal nuggets, and rare alchemical bottles.
* Crates bob along the waves and can drift out to open ocean if not secured.

---

## 🎣 The Flotsam Hook
* **Recipe**: `2x Sticks, 2x String, 1x Iron Nugget`
* **Usage**: Cast your grappling hook toward drifting crates. When the hook latches on, reel it back in to pull the crate directly to the beach!

---

## 🧺 Sieve Basket
* **Recipe**: `4x Sticks, 2x Oak Planks, 1x String`
* **Usage**: Right-click on Sand or Gravel blocks with the Sieve Basket in hand.
* **Drops**:
  * Flint
  * Clay Balls
  * Iron Nuggets
  * Copper Nuggets
  * **Ocean Pearls** (Rare material used for ancient totems and deep diving gear)
